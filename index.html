03 · The playbook

The prescription: 7 moves

Fix the holes in order. Each move makes the next one stronger.

01
The offer

Sell certainty, not plumbing

A homeowner calling a plumber is not price-shopping. They are scared — scared of being ripped off, of the job done wrong, of a mess left behind. The business that kills that fear wins the job, even at higher prices.

The "No-Surprise Guarantee"

The safest choice in town — not the cheapest.
  • Same-day service · 60-minute emergency response
  • Upfront flat pricing — the quoted price is the final price
  • Fixed right or we come back free — 2-year labor warranty
  • Free whole-home plumbing inspection ($199 value) with every job
  • Spotless cleanup — shoe covers, drop cloths, left cleaner than we found it
02
Pricing power

Raise prices 20–30%

+20–30%on every job — funded by the guarantee, not by hope.

Premium price funds premium everything: better techs, faster response, nicer trucks, bigger ad budget. The cheap competitors literally cannot afford to copy you. You are not charging enough — the guarantee is what lets you charge what you're worth.

03
Value equation

Maximize the top, crush the bottom

Value = (Dream Outcome × Certainty) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort). Push the top up, drive the bottom to zero:

Dream outcome×Certainty
Time delayEffort
Dream outcome → problem gone TODAY.
Certainty → reviews, before-and-after photos, the guarantee in writing.
Time delay → killed by same-day service + 60-minute response.
Effort → killed by "we handle everything" — text updates, online booking, zero mess.
04
Lead engine

All four buckets, firing

Most plumbers run one channel and pray. Run all four — warm, referrals, partners, paid — and answer every call in under 60 seconds. In emergency plumbing, the first business to answer usually wins the job.

WARM — past customers"Free plumbing health check this month" · cheapest leads you'll ever get
REFERRALS — the reframeFree drain inspection for a neighbor · "who should we check next?"
PARTNERS — realtors & property managersOne relationship can feed you jobs for years
PAID — Google + MetaEmergency keywords = highest intent on earth · Meta for heaters & plans
⚡ SPEED-TO-LEADUnder 60 seconds, 24/7 — this alone is free money

Never ask "give me referrals." Offer the free inspection — the referral asks itself.

05
Recurring revenue

Turn jobs into relationships

Annual plan
$199/yr

Home Plumbing Protection Plan

Yearly inspection + priority booking + 10% off all repairs. A $400 one-time job becomes a $400-a-year relationship — the base that survives slow months.

The rule
BAM-FAM

Book a meeting from a meeting

Tech finishes the job, then books the water-heater flush or annual inspection before walking out the door. Never leave without the next booking.

06
List building

The giveaway play

Once a quarter: "Win a free water heater + professional install ($2,500 value)." Every entrant gets a free inspection. Win or lose, you just built a list of homeowners in your service area — and announcing the prize value kills sticker shock before the sales conversation even starts.

The one-liner
